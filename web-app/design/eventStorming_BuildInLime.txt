::Events simulation for the single use case::
#Create a Unit to be built:[pic of the sample Cob Oven to be built]
Client creates Unit(COB Oven, status - Open)
Client updates Unit(with new description, timeline - 7 days, automatically status is updated to onTrack, backLog or delayed based on timeline, elapsed time and status of constituent tasks)
Client creates Team(COB Oven Team) for the Unit 
Client updates Team(with new members)
Client creates Channels(Requirement, Design, Finance, Experimentation, Materials, Execution) for the Team
Client updates Channels(by adding specific members to each channel)

#Create a requirement document:[document with the requirement specs]
Client opens Task(make initial requirement doc, status - Open) in Channel(Requirement)
Client updates Task(by assigning himself and architect to it, , timeline - 2 days, automatically status is updated to onTrack, backLog or delayed based on timeline and elapsed time)
Client updates Task(by adding requirements doc, asking for feedback from architect)
Architect updates Task(by adding his feedback)
Client updates Task(by accommodating architects feedback)
Architect signs-off Task
Client signs-off Task 
Client closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Create a design document:[document with design specs]
Architect opens Task(make initial design doc, status - Open) in Channel(Design)
Architect updates Task(by assigning himself, supervisor and client to it, timeline - 2 days, automatically status is updated to onTrack, backLog or delayed based on timeline and elapsed time)
Architect updates Task(by adding design doc, asking for feedback from client and supervisor)
Client updates Task(by adding his feedback)
Supervisor updates Task(by adding his feedback)
Architect updates Task(by accommodating feedback from client and supervisor)
Client signs-off Task
Supervisor signs-off Task 
Architect closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Make Soil Samples for cob oven:[table of soil sample observations, pics of sample block of soil]
Supervisor opens Task(make four soil samples spread across three different temperature ranges, status - Open) in Channel(Experimentation)
Supervisor updates Task(by assigning architect and head mason to it, timeline - 2 days)
Supervisor updates Task(by adding a table of data form the observations, asks for feedback from architect)
Architect updates Task(by adding his feedback and next steps to be taken)
Supervisor updates Task(by accommodating the feedback)
Architect signs-off Task
Supervisor signs-off Task
Supervisor closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Make a budget for Cob oven:[table with budget allocation and expected costs]
Client opens Task(make a table with budget allocation, status - Open) in Channel(Finance)
Client updates Task(by assigning himself and architect to it, , timeline - 1 day, automatically status is updated to onTrack, backLog or delayed based on timeline and elapsed time)
Client updates Task(by adding more information based on vendor research, asks for feedback from architect)
Architect updates Task(by adding his feedback)
Client updates Task(by accommodating architects feedback)

#Procure Materials Required with a bill for the same:[table of materials procured, bills and pic]
Supervisor opens Task(fetch all the materials required for cob oven, status - Open) in Channel(Materials)
Supervisor updates Task(by assigning architect and Client to it, timeline - 2 days)
Supervisor updates Task(by adding a table of data specifying the materials required, asks for feedback from architect, Client)
Architect updates Task(by adding his feedback)
Client updates Task(by adding his notes on materials required)
Supervisor updates Task(by accommodating the feedback and fetching the material)
Architect signs-off Task
Supervisor signs-off Task
Client signs-off Task
Supervisor closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Make Cob Oven and Share the pictures or video:[pic and/or video of the cob oven from the site]
Supervisor opens Task(make cob oven based on the design, status - Open) in Channel(Execution)
Supervisor updates Task(by assigning architect, Client and Head mason to it, timeline - 2 days)
Supervisor updates Task(by adding pics of the cob oven built, asks for feedback from architect and Client)
Architect updates Task(by adding his feedback and next steps to be taken)
Client updates Task(by providing his feedback)
Supervisor updates Task(by accommodating the feedback and sharing the new pics)
Architect signs-off Task
Client signs-off Task
Supervisor signs-off Task
Supervisor closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Make a budget for Cob oven:[table with budget allocation and expected costs]
Client updates Task(by adding actual expenses based on inputs from supervisor)
Architect signs-off Task
Client signs-off Task 
Client closes Task(automatically status - Closed)

#Create a Unit to be built:[pic and/or of the Cob Oven being built from the site]
Client closes Channels(General, Design, Finance, Experimentation, Materials, Execution, statuses - closed)
Client closes Team(COB Oven Team, status - closed) for the Unit
Client closes Unit(COB Oven, status - closed)
