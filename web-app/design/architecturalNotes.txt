 - Bounded context:
    - It is to allow different parts of the application which are different from business/culture/data point of view to evolve independently
    - Based on the current observation and understanding of the domain, these are some of them - 
      1) Admin - deals with stakeholders involved; their identity, privacy and security
                 Example relevant queries:
		  - what is my profile picture?
		  - what are all the projects I am involved in?
		  - who can see my profile ?
                  - who are my team mates ?
		  - who are my project mates ?
		  - where can I download all my personal data ?
		  - how can I download all my projects ?
		  - Do I allow data of my interaction in application for the analytics ?
      2) Organization - deals with different entities and their organization/relationship in the system
                  Example relevant queries:
		   - how many build-units are in this project ?
		   - how many com-channels are there in this build-unit ?
		   - how many teams are involved in this project ?
		   - how many man-hours are used for this build-unit ?
		   - how much time was spent to build this build-unit ?
		   - how was the time spent across different comm-channels for this build-unit ?
      3) Comm-Channel - deals with communication between stakeholders (primarily chat) in a particular domain of construction like Finance, Execution,
                        Experimentation, Design, Requirements, Materials, Tools
			Example relevant queries:
			 - how frequently was the conversation going on for execution of this build-unit ?
			 - can you provide the summary of decisions taken during the designing of this build-unit?
			 - can you provide the final picture of the build-unit after construction?
      4) AI-Support - deals with all the AI assistance required for comm-channels
                        Example relevant queries:
			 - summarize the communication happened while execution of this build-unit ?
			 - can you check if the walls are sufficiently wet for lime plastering based on the pictures in exection comm-channel of a particular build-unit?
			 - Can you provide the summary of expenses for this build-unit?
			 - Can you provide the table of experimentation done for cob-oven build-unit ?
 - User Story based on the bounded contexts defined above:
    *note on convention: Bounded Context involved are specified in small braces, Artifacts involved are specified in square braces
    - Build a basement for our Project:
      1) Create profiles of people involved in construction (#Admin)
         - Sign-up with BuildInLime application as a client
	 - create a project and populate its properties like overview, timeline, location, category(commercial, household, institution, farmhouse etc), 
	   type(stone, brick, mixed, mud etc)
	 - add a team of individuals to the project
      2) Create a build-unit and relevant comm-channels for the basement (#Organization | #Comm-Channel)
         - create a build-unit (Basement in this case)
	 - add members (architect, supervisor, head-mason etc ) involved in this build-unit, picked from the project team 
	 - create all the relevant comm-channels of this build-unit (Finance, Design, Execution, Requirements, Materials, Tools)
	 - assign members to comm-channels
	 - populate high level properties of the build-unit like timeline, priority, status, overview, type(stone, brick, cement etc) 
      3) Update requirements (#Comm-Channel | #AI-Support)[requirement document]
         - client discusses about basement (based on his own research) with architect (to align with overall house design) and supervisor (to align with current local conditions)
	 - client finalizes the requirement in a doc and asks for sign-off by architect
	 - architect signs-off after reviewing the doc
	 - client signs-off the document
	 - Requirements phase is marked complete/green
      4) Update design (#Comm-Channel)[Design document]
         - architect updates the initial design doc based on the house plan
	 - discusses with client and supervisor (regarding local conditions) on any issues
	 - client signs-off the design
	 - architect marks it complete/green
      5) Update Materials (#Comm-Channel | #AI-Support)[document of market options, procurement schedule, final material inventory details]
         - supervisor updates the list of materials required in text/audio format and also relevant notes on market research
	 - generates excel sheet with the help of AI-Assistant
	 - assigns client and architect to review the same
	 - client discusses available market options (with details of quality and cost) with supervisor/architect
	 - once discussion is complete, supervisor generates the final sheet with details of procurement
	 - client and architect reviews the same and signs-off the procurement schedule
	 - supervisor starts procurement and once the procurement is complete, generates final sheet with actual details of 
	   materials in inventory(quality, quantity, cost and special notes) with the help of AI-Assistant
	 - supervisor marks the procurement phase complete/green
	 5.1) Update Finance (#Comm-Channel | #AI-Support)[document with updated expenditure on materials and inventory details]
              - client updates the overall budget with expenditure information from Materials channel
      6) Update execution (#Comm-Channel | #AI-Support)[document with details of work hours, man hours, local issues]
         - supervisor adds head mason to the channel
	 - supervisor updates details of expected duration, labor involved and any local issues in text/audio
	 - any specifics are discussed in the channel with client and architect
	 - daily progress info is shared as audio/image/video
	 - updates are annotated with stars (on a scale of 5 to qualify their importance)
	 - important updates which need to be highlighted are annotated with 'important' symbol
	 - supervisor updates final pic or video of the basement constructed
	 - supervisor updates actual details of duration, labor(man-hours), material usage in text/audio/video format
	 - supervisor generates summary doc with execution details with AI-Assistant
	 - client/architect reviews the doc and signs-off
	 - supervisor marks the execution complete/green
         6.1) Update Finance (#Comm-Channel | #AI-Support)[document with details of expenditure on labor and man-hours]
	      - client updates the expenditure on labor
      7) Update build-unit (#Organization | #AI-Support)[summary document of the build unit with details requirement, design, materials and execution]
         - client generates the summary of build-unit with AI-Assistant
	 - client marks the build-unit complete/green
